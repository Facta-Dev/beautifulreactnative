# /domain-modeling

## Delegate

Delegate to the **domain-modeling** skill. Activate when the request changes
the `<app>` domain model itself: a term is disputed or overloaded, a new
concept is being named, or a decision worth recording is being made. The
skill challenges the fuzzy wording, cross-checks the claim against the code,
updates `GLOSSARY.md` in place, and writes an ADR only when the decision is
hard to reverse, surprising without context, and the result of a real
trade-off.
